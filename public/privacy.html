Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Nitro Outreach ("Nitro Outreach," "we," "us," or "our") provides an all-in-one marketing workspace where you can build websites, generate content, schedule social posts, plan ad campaigns, and manage related work. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the service, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Business profile — details you provide during onboarding or in settings, such as company name, industry, website, goals, and brand colors.
  • Content you create — websites, posts, captions, campaign plans, and the prompts or topics you enter to generate them.
  • Usage data — actions in the workspace, feature usage, and counts such as AI generations used.
  • Payment information — if you subscribe to a paid plan, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number; we store limited subscription details (plan, status, and identifiers returned by Stripe).
  • Connected accounts — if you connect a social account (such as Instagram), we store the access token and basic account identifiers needed to publish on your behalf.
  • Cookies — a session cookie is used to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

2. How we use your information

  • Provide, operate, and secure the service and your workspace.
  • Generate content and answer requests using AI, based on the context you provide.
  • Process subscriptions and payments.
  • Publish scheduled posts to social accounts you have connected.
  • Send transactional emails such as verification, password resets, and account notices.
  • Improve reliability, troubleshoot issues, and prevent abuse.

3. Instagram and Meta data

If you choose to connect an Instagram (or Facebook) account, we request only the permissions needed to publish content you schedule and to identify the connected account. We use this access solely to publish posts you create and to show your connection status. We store the access token securely and use it only for these purposes. You can disconnect at any time, which removes our ability to publish and deletes the stored token. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings, which removes your workspace data. You may also request deletion or a copy of your data by contacting us. Some records may be retained where required for legal, security, or accounting reasons.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access, update, or correct your information in Settings.
  • Disconnect connected social accounts at any time.
  • Delete your account, or request deletion or export of your data.
  • Opt out of non-essential emails using the controls in your notification settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. Contact us to exercise them.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including password hashing, encrypted connections, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Nitro Outreach is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

We operate from the United States and may process and store information there. By using the service, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy or your data? Email nitrooutreach@outlook.com.

This policy describes our current practices and may be updated as the service evolves.
